Frequently Asked Questions

Questions & Answers

Where is Dr Suleiman Murisho located?

Dr Suleiman Murisho is based in Nairobi, Kenya. Please contact us before visiting for consultation and location details.

How can I book a consultation?

You can request a consultation by phone, WhatsApp, email or through the contact form on this website.

Are consultations private?

Consultations are intended to be respectful and private. Avoid sharing highly sensitive information through public channels.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Traditional or spiritual consultations cannot guarantee a particular outcome.

Can traditional consultation replace medical care?

No. Medical, pregnancy, fertility and other health concerns should also be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
