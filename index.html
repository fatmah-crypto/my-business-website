FAQ

Where is Dr Suleimain Murisho located? +
Dr Suleimain Murisho is based in Nairobi, Kenya. Contact us before visiting for consultation and location details.
How do I book a consultation? +
Use the phone, WhatsApp, email or contact form on this website to request a consultation.
Are consultations private? +
Consultations are intended to be respectful and private. Please avoid sharing highly sensitive information through public channels.
Are results guaranteed? +
No. Traditional or spiritual consultations cannot guarantee a particular outcome.
Can traditional consultation replace medical care? +
No. For medical, mental-health, pregnancy, fertility or other health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional. Traditional consultation may be considered separately as cultural or spiritual support.
